How to connect
  1. Add Morrow to your assistant, then open it. Use Morrow with one assistant at a time.
  2. Open a Canvas or Moodle course in Chrome and sign in.
  3. Select Connect Morrow, then Connect course.
  4. Ask Morrow for help in the assistant you chose.

Chrome will ask for access to the course site you select. If Canvas New Quizzes is open, it may also ask for access to that quiz site.

Disconnect Morrow stops this connection and removes its course site access. It does not sign you out or undo course changes.
