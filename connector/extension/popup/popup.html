How to connect
  1. Add Morrow to your assistant, then open it. Use Morrow with one assistant at a time.
  2. Open a Canvas course in Chrome and sign in.
  3. Select Connect Morrow, then Connect Canvas course.
  4. Ask Morrow for help in the assistant you chose.

Chrome will ask for access to the Canvas site you select. If New Quizzes is open, it may also ask for access to that quiz site.

Disconnect Morrow stops this connection and removes its Canvas site access. It does not sign you out of Canvas or undo course changes.
