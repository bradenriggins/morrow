What Morrow Bridge can read

After you agree and connect a course, Morrow Bridge reads the Canvas or Moodle pages and course content needed for your requests. Course content can include names, email addresses, and messages. It sends the needed course details through the Morrow app on this computer to the assistant you choose. Your Chrome password and cookies stay in Chrome. Privacy details

Select Agree and continue to accept this data use. Morrow Bridge will not connect to Morrow or read course data before you agree.

How to connect
  1. Choose your assistant in Morrow. Open the Morrow app and follow the action it shows.
  2. Finish Morrow Bridge setup. Add Morrow Bridge in Chrome, review and accept the course data disclosure, select Connect Morrow, and allow only the connection you started.
  3. Open and connect your course. Open a signed-in Canvas or Moodle course. Morrow Bridge detects the platform and shows Connect Canvas or Connect Moodle. Select that button, then choose courses in Plan and Edit settings.

Chrome asks for access only to the Canvas or Moodle address you connect. If Canvas New Quizzes is open, it may also ask for access to that quiz address.

Disconnect Morrow stops this connection and removes that platform access. It does not sign you out or undo course changes.

Chrome works with one Morrow at a time. A second Morrow reports that another Morrow is already using this connection, and it starts without its course tools. Close the other Morrow, or use one Morrow for all your assistants.
