Morrow setup

Follow one next action at a time. You can close this guide and return at any time.

Checking setup

Morrow is checking this connection.

  • Assistant approval is not checked
  • Morrow Bridge connection is not checked
  • Morrow version is not checked
  • Course connection is not checked
  • First read is not checked

Open Morrow

Open Morrow, choose your assistant, then return to Morrow Bridge.

Open the Morrow app and choose your assistant. Then return to Morrow Bridge and select Connect Morrow.

Three setup stages

  1. Choose your assistant in Morrow

    Open the Morrow app and choose the assistant you use.

    Chrome works with one Morrow at a time. A second Morrow reports that another Morrow is already using this connection, and it starts without its course tools. Close the other Morrow, or use one Morrow for all your assistants.

  2. Finish Morrow Bridge setup

    Follow the Bridge actions in the Morrow app. Then open Morrow Bridge, select Connect Morrow, and allow only the connection you started.

  3. Open and connect your course

    Open a signed-in Canvas or Moodle course in Chrome. Morrow Bridge identifies the platform and shows Connect Canvas or Connect Moodle. Select that button, choose courses in Plan, then complete the first read that Morrow shows.

Plan keeps changes in review

Connecting a course selects it in Plan. A change in Plan waits for your review. You choose if and when to grant Edit access later in Plan and Edit settings.
