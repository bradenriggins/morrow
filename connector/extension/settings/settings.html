Access rules

  • Plan keeps changes ready for your review.
  • Edit applies only to the selected courses and checked actions.
  • Return a course to Plan to remove saved Edit access immediately.
  • Edit access saved here ends after the duration you choose. The default is one hour. Edit access requested in a Morrow conversation ends after 30 minutes.
  • Some actions cannot be checked after they are saved. Morrow marks them here and reports the saved result as unconfirmed.
  • If a course needs sign-in again, open its site in Chrome and reconnect it from the Morrow popup.
