# Teacher workflows

Use [the doctrine](DOCTRINE.md), the relevant provider contract, and current
course evidence. Separate preparation from live delivery. Never treat a request
to draft content as permission to publish it, message learners, or change grades.

## Course setup and launch

Inventory structure, syllabus, outcomes, schedule, assessment groups, grading
rules, navigation, support contacts, accessibility, and learner prerequisites.
Check the first learner route: entry → orientation → first activity → submission
→ feedback. Identify hidden/unpublished objects, date restrictions, broken
links, missing files, and external-tool dependencies. “The teacher can open it”
is not a student-access test. Do not enroll a test student or impersonate one
without explicit authority and a supported path.

## Semester rollover

Confirm source and destination courses, term, time zone, teaching schedule,
holidays, and whether the course already has enrollments or submissions. Read
before copying or shifting dates. Compare assignment overrides, module dates,
availability windows, quiz settings, completion criteria, file links, and LTI
links. Preserve accommodation overrides and institutional templates. Do not
copy learner records, discussions, grades, or personal submissions as content.
A successful import does not prove the new course is ready; verify target
objects and learner route. Large transfers may be asynchronous: wait for the
provider's final state and inspect errors before reporting completion.

## Weekly maintenance

Find missing dates or instructions, inconsistent names, unreachable resources,
and unclear next steps. Prioritize blockers over cosmetic changes. Produce a
small change list bound to exact objects. Verify saved results and preserve
unrelated material. Use a common rubric across many courses, with a separate
result for each course and incomplete coverage clearly marked.

## Assessment and feedback

Draft a clear task, aligned criteria, points, submission requirements, feedback,
and examples of quality. Check due and close dates in the course time zone,
including daylight-saving transitions. Preserve accommodations and existing
attempts. Read [write hazards](write-hazards.md) before points or rubric changes.
Do not infer a grade, misconduct finding, or accommodation eligibility from a
single automated signal. The educator makes consequential learner decisions.

Feedback should be specific: identify the evidence, explain the gap against
criteria, and suggest a next action. Use only the approved minimum student data.
Drafting messages is different from sending them. Canvas excludes
announcements and conversation sends; provide a draft for the educator to use.
For Moodle, read [its workflows](moodle-workflows.md) and the operation's
current admission before any forum post or notification-related change.
Do not include other learners' records in feedback.

## End of term

Check outstanding grading, incomplete work, approved extensions, retention
rules, and export needs. Concluding or deleting a course is distinct from
hiding a module. Respect evidence-held operations even when requested. Provide
a manual plan when a supported governed operation does not exist; never run an
arbitrary API call or UI sequence to bypass the hold.
