Checking setup

Set up Morrow on this computer

Connect your assistant to Canvas and Moodle. Build, review, and update courses through conversation.

On Windows, Morrow installs for your user account and does not ask for administrator approval. If managed-device policy blocks it, ask your IT team to deploy Morrow.

On a Mac, Morrow sets up inside your user account and does not ask for an administrator password. If managed-device policy blocks it, ask your IT team to deploy Morrow.
