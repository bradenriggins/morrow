## Design Context

`docs/brand/MORROW-BRAND.md` is the single source for Morrow's type, color,
wordmark, voice, and claim rules. Read it before changing any Morrow surface.
This file carries only the audience, the working defaults, and the design
principles. Where the two differ, `docs/brand/MORROW-BRAND.md` is correct.

### Users

Morrow serves instructors, instructional designers, accessibility specialists,
and the people who administer courses. They use their chosen assistant to
inspect and update course work. They should not need to understand browser
connections or server internals. Connection and review screens must answer what
is connected, what will change, and what happens next.

### Brand Personality

Calm, clear, capable. Use plain language, specific actions, and honest results.
Keep the LMS terms that users already know. Do not add claims of perfection. Do
not call Morrow an "AI app".

### Aesthetic Direction

The September 6, 2026 revision replaces the September 4 direction. Use Manrope,
cool white surfaces, navy text, cobalt actions, and a small chartreuse highlight
where it has a clear purpose. Do not use a serif display font, earth tones,
parchment, gradient text, or glowing dark panels.

Take exact color values from the table in `docs/brand/MORROW-BRAND.md`. Do not
copy them into this file, and do not introduce a value that is not in
`connector/extension/brand/theme.css`.

Use the Morrow knot, `connector/extension/brand/morrow-knot.svg`, with a
live-text "morrow" wordmark beside it. Place the knot directly on the
surrounding surface. Do not add a tile, shadow, border, or radius behind it.

Apply this direction to the extension, the installer, the pairing and review
screens, the website, and the video. Support both themes, narrow windows,
keyboard use, reduced motion, and forced colors.

### Implementation Defaults

Keep the existing HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and server-rendered HTML. Use native
buttons, forms, and details. `connector/extension/brand/theme.css` is the token
source for every Morrow-owned surface: import it where the surface can, and
otherwise copy its token names and values without change. Use its semantic
tokens, such as `--text`, `--accent`, `--success`, and `--danger`, rather than
literal color values. Do not add a UI framework or dependencies.

### Design Principles

1. Show the next useful action before technical details.
2. Keep permission, changes, consequences, and uncertainty visible.
3. Distinguish an active Morrow connection from a checked Canvas connection.
4. Preserve exact requested values. Keep technical records in closed details.
5. Use spacing and weight for hierarchy. Keep the established colors and density.
