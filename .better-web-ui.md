## Design Context

### Users

Morrow serves instructors, course designers, and Canvas administrators. They use
their chosen AI application to inspect and update courses. They should not need
to understand browser connections or server internals. Connection and review
screens must answer what is connected, what will change, and what happens next.

### Brand Personality

Calm, clear, capable. Use plain language, specific actions, and honest results.
Keep Canvas terms that users already know. Do not add claims of perfection.

### Aesthetic Direction

Use the original Morrow wordmark with its orbit logo and Google Sans Flex.
Braden replaced the cream direction on September 4, 2026: use crisp white,
cool neutral surfaces, strong graphite text, and violet actions. Do not use a
serif display font. Apply this direction to the connector, pairing and review
screens, website, and video. Support both themes, narrow windows, keyboard use,
reduced motion, and forced colors.

Light colors: paper and surface `#ffffff`, sunken surface `#f5f6fa`, border
`#e3e6ee`, text `#171923`, secondary text `#5f6375`, accent `#4931aa`, and
text on accent `#ffffff`. Dark colors: paper `#101117`, surface `#191b25`,
text `#f4f4f7`, accent `#bfb2ff`, and text on accent `#171923`.

Use the original transparent black wordmark on light surfaces and its white
variant on dark surfaces. Do not recreate it with normal text beside an icon.

### Implementation Defaults

Keep the existing HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and server-rendered HTML. Use native
buttons, forms, and details. Shared tokens live in
`connector/extension/brand/theme.css`. Do not add a UI framework or dependencies.

### Design Principles

1. Show the next useful action before technical details.
2. Keep permission, changes, consequences, and uncertainty visible.
3. Distinguish an active Morrow connection from a checked Canvas connection.
4. Preserve exact requested values. Keep technical records in closed details.
5. Use spacing and weight for hierarchy. Keep the established colors and density.
